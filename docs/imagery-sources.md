The new photographs are illustrative and do not depict Monarch staff or client projects. Each was visually inspected at its intended crop and exported as a local WebP asset. The source photographs are marked free to use under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

Retained: the layered hero, cloud passage, door, generated mountain workspace, closing landscape, brand logos, real founder portraits, and work thumbnails tied to their projects. Their subjects and narrative roles were more appropriate than stock replacements.

Candidates rejected after inspection included a neon editing desk (off palette), a storyboard whose subject disappeared in the wide crop, generic laptop desks, and several barren paths that lost the site's foliage rich atmosphere.
