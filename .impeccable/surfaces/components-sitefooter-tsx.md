## Direction contract
THESIS: A landscape-backed editorial sign-off, not another hero or an animation gate.
OWN-WORLD: Warm cream dissolves into the existing bridge landscape. Forest type above, cream utility type below; Tiempos statement and Space Grotesk navigation. No new assets or palette.
STORY: A visitor finishes the page, sees one invitation, and can immediately contact Monarch or choose a page. No repeated authority pitch or legal placeholders.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned two-line editorial statement and pill action at upper left; landscape breathes at right. Lower area places logo/email left and two plain navigation groups right, with a quiet bottom copyright row. Content-sized, not pinned.
FORM: Local component redesign, code-led within the existing system. No concept seed required for this scoped implementation of approved critique.
MOTION: A single gentle image-settling entrance plus viewport-local text reveals. Never hide navigation or defer focus. Reduced-motion static; no scroll handlers or continuous loops.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
